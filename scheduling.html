Meeting Reminders
Reminders for meetings scheduled from the Inbox's calendar icon (each coach's own Zoom/calendar, per contact). "Minutes before" accepts a comma-separated list -- e.g. "1440, 60" sends one reminder a day before and another an hour before. Available merge tags: {{firstName}} {{lastName}} {{coachName}} {{date}} {{time}} {{duration}}.
